Welcome to My Fit AI, developed by CTT Labs. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our users in compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, GDPR, and CCPA.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When signing in with Google, we receive your display name, email address, and profile photo via Google Authentication.
  • Workout & Fitness Data: Workout logs, exercise history, sets, reps, weight loads, 1RM calculations, and rest times. All workout data is stored locally in an encrypted Room SQLite database on your device.
  • Nutrition & Body Measurements: Calorie intake, protein/carb/fat logs, body weight, and biometric measurements.
  • Photos (Camera Access): Progress posture photos and food pictures captured by the user are stored strictly locally on the device's sandboxed storage and are never transmitted to our servers.

2. Device Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers real-time workout rest timer notifications and daily training reminders.
  • CAMERA: Allows optional user capture of body posture pictures and meal logs.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE: Maintains an active countdown timer on your lock screen during workout rest intervals.
  • VIBRATE: Provides tactile feedback when completing exercise sets.

3. Third-Party Services

My Fit AI integrates verified third-party SDKs to provide essential features:

  • Google Play Services & Google Identity: For secure authentication. (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays banner and rewarded ads using pseudonymous identifiers. (AdMob Policy)
  • Cloudflare AI Proxy: Stateless processing of natural language fitness tips without storing personal identities. (Cloudflare Policy)

4. Data Protection & Security

We employ industry-standard security safeguards. All network communication occurs exclusively over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections (Cleartext HTTP traffic is strictly disabled in production builds). We do not sell or monetize personal user data.

5. Children's Privacy

My Fit AI is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Account & Data Deletion Instructions

Google Play Account Deletion Policy Compliance

In compliance with Google Play's Data Safety and Account Deletion policies, My Fit AI provides two straightforward methods to delete your account and all associated personal data:

Method 1: Direct In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)
  1. Open the My Fit AI app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Cài đặt (Settings) icon on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to Sao lưu & Quản lý dữ liệu (Backup & Data).
  4. Tap "Xóa toàn bộ dữ liệu & Tài khoản" (Delete Account & All Data) and confirm.
  5. All your local workout history, nutrition logs, photos, and cached authentication tokens will be immediately and irreversibly purged from your device.
Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, submit a deletion request by email:

  • To: ctt.labs@gmail.com
  • Subject: Request Account and Data Deletion - My Fit
  • Content: Provide the Google account email address you used to sign in to My Fit.

Our support team will process your request and permanently erase all cloud-associated records within 48 business hours.
