## 2. Inherent Physical Risk of Exercise

Engaging in strenuous physical exercise, strength training, resistance weightlifting, and high-intensity interval training (HIIT) carries inherent physical risks. These risks include, but are not limited to:

- Muscle tears, joint strains, sprains, and fractures.
- Cardiovascular strain, sudden changes in blood pressure, heart palpitations, or fainting.
- Chronic overuse injuries resulting from improper lifting form, excessive weight loads, or inadequate recovery periods.

**You voluntarily assume full responsibility for your physical safety, posture form, and well-being when performing any exercise listed or suggested in the App.**

## 3. Mandatory Medical Clearance

You should obtain medical clearance from a licensed physician before beginning any workout program or diet plan with My Fit AI, especially if you:
- Are over 35 years of age and currently sedentary.
- Have a history of heart disease, hypertension, cardiovascular disorders, or stroke.
- Experience recurring chest pain, dizziness, vertigo, or loss of balance during physical exertion.
- Have pre-existing orthopedic conditions, spinal disc herniation, arthritis, or prior joint surgeries.
- Are pregnant, nursing, or managing metabolic conditions such as Type 1 or Type 2 Diabetes.

## 4. Emergency Medical Situations

If you experience any of the following symptoms while exercising:
- Acute chest pain, tightness, or pressure radiating to the neck, jaw, or arm.
- Severe shortness of breath, hyperventilation, or lightheadedness.
- Nausea, cold sweats, or sudden loss of vision.

**STOP EXERCISING IMMEDIATELY AND CALL YOUR LOCAL EMERGENCY NUMBER (115 in Vietnam, 911 in the USA, 112 in the EU) OR SEEK IMMEDIATE MEDICAL ATTENTION.**
